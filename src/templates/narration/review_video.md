## Instructions

Write narration that:
1. Opens with genuine praise for what works well
2. Transitions naturally to opportunities: "Building on this strength..."
3. Describes the potential impact of suggested improvements
4. Maintains an encouraging, expert tone throughout
5. Matches the visual content being shown

## Anti-Patterns — Do NOT:
- Lead with criticism before acknowledging strengths
- Use negative framing: "lacks", "fails to", "poor"
- Sound generic — reference specific visual elements being shown
- Start with "In this section" or "Now we see"

Output ONLY the narration text — no JSON, no markdown formatting, just the spoken words.
Do NOT include any preamble such as "Here is the narration:" — start directly with the spoken words.
